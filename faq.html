A few things you might be wondering about the panel and what comes next.

01When will the panel launch?

We’re targeting March 7, 2027 at midnight in the visitor’s local timezone for the BT Panel launch. The homepage countdown shows the current target date.

02What games will be supported?

BT Panel is being built for popular dedicated game servers. The supported title list and compatibility details will be confirmed before launch.

03Is there a free tier?

Plan and license details are still being finalized. We’ll share the confirmed options before launch.

04Can I self-host?

Deployment options are under development. We’ll publish the requirements and setup guidance before release.

05What about backups?

Backup capabilities and storage options are being finalized and will be documented with the release.

06How do I get support?

Support and community channels will be announced before launch. The current preview does not send or store contact-form submissions.
